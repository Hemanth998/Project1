Interesting facts about Svalbard.

Svalbard is an Archepelago in the Arctic Ocean, Is territory of Norway, And is known for its High Polar bear population, It's extremely cold as its located within arctic circle so the permanent population is less than 2000. Its capital is Longyearbyen which is the largest settlement in the Svalbard. No Visa required to stay and work in Svalbard, however to reach Svalbard through Norway, a Schengen visa is required.
